Privacy Policy

HypeCheck is a small personal app for pausing before a purchase. This page says plainly what it keeps about you and why.

What we keep

  • Your account: your name and email address. If you use a password, we keep only a scrambled (scrypt-hashed) version of it, never the password itself.
  • If you sign in with Google: Google tells us your name, your email address, and an ID number for your Google account. We do not see your Google password, contacts, files, or anything else.
  • What you put in: your wishes (name, price, notes, product link), your decisions and reflections, your expenses, and your monthly budgets.
  • Sign-in records: a record of each signed-in session, and failed sign-in attempts with the network address they came from. Failed attempts are deleted after 15 minutes. They exist only to stop password guessing.

What we do with it

We use it to run the app for you: to show you your own wishes and spending, and to keep other people out of your account. That is all.

  • No advertising, no analytics trackers, no selling or renting of your information. Ever.
  • Other users of HypeCheck cannot see your wishes, expenses, budgets or decisions.
  • We do not send marketing email.

Cookies

One cookie, hc_school_session, keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. It cannot be read by scripts and is deleted when you sign out. While you sign in with Google, a second cookie exists for up to 10 minutes to make sure the sign-in belongs to your browser. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.

Where it is stored

This school edition runs locally on this computer and stores its data in MySQL. Local connections use the loopback address. Google sign-in is optional and only available when configured; Google handles that step. The personal hosted edition uses Vercel and Neon over HTTPS.

How long we keep it

Your account and what you put in stay until you delete them or ask us to. Sessions end after 30 days or when you sign out.

Your choices

  • You can edit or remove your wishes and expenses in the app at any time. Decided wishes stay in your history.
  • You can export your monthly expenses as a CSV file from the Spending screen.
  • To get a copy of everything, correct something, or delete your account and all its data, email ghiankyledomingo2007@gmail.com. We will do it within 30 days. If you are in the Philippines, these are your rights under the Data Privacy Act of 2012.
  • To disconnect Google, remove HypeCheck in your Google Account connections.

Children

HypeCheck is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly keep their information.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the date at the top of this page changes.
